## 2026-07-31 - Keyboard Navigation & Screen Reader States in Custom Carousels
**Learning:** For highly interactive slider/carousel widgets, pointer & wheel events are insufficient for universal accessibility. Screen readers must be shielded from off-screen slides via `aria-hidden={i !== activeIndex}`, while keyboard-focused users need explicit Arrow Key listeners coupled with `focus-visible` styles on the region container. Real-time assertive elements (like countdowns) must use `aria-live="assertive"` and `aria-atomic="true"` to prevent screen readers from missing crucial fast-changing values.
**Action:** Always wrap custom viewport regions with appropriate interactive roles, focus indices, and screen reader visibility gates to guarantee a fully compliant and delightful micro-UX.

## 2025-05-14 - [Keyboard Shortcuts for Critical Interactions]
**Learning:** For a real-time recording application, providing immediate keyboard access (Space for Start/Stop, Arrows for Prompt selection) significantly reduces friction and makes the app feel more native and accessible. Adding subtle visual hints for these shortcuts aids discoverability without cluttering the UI.
**Action:** Always consider keyboard shortcuts for the "happy path" of time-sensitive or repetitive interactions, and provide visual hints that appear on desktop but stay hidden or secondary on mobile.

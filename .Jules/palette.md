## 2025-05-15 - Keyboard shortcuts for practice flow
**Learning:** Adding keyboard shortcuts to the core recording flow (Arrows for navigation, Enter to start, Space to stop) significantly reduces friction and improves accessibility for power users.
**Action:** Always include visual hints (e.g., "(Enter)") and proper ARIA attributes (aria-keyshortcuts) when adding keyboard shortcuts to buttons. Use `e.key` and `e.preventDefault()` for consistent and safe handling.

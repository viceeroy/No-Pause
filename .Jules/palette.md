## 2025-05-15 - [Visual Silence Nudge]
**Learning:** In a real-time speech analysis app where users are penalized for hesitations, providing a visual "pre-penalty" warning (silence nudge) can significantly reduce user anxiety and help them stay "in flow" before they hit a scoring penalty.
**Action:** Always look for opportunities to provide proactive visual feedback for time-sensitive constraints, ideally giving the user a brief window to correct their behavior before a negative consequence (like a score drop) occurs.
